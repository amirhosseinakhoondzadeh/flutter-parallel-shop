SHOP-41 Store money as integer cents

Money is a double in euros across the app. Checkout turns the cart total
into cents with `(total * 100).toInt()`, and for a €19.99 cart that sends
1998 to the payment provider instead of 1999.

Store prices and totals as integer cents end to end, so checkout sends the
exact amount. What the shopper sees on screen must not change.

Done when: a €19.99 cart sends 1999 from paymentAmount, every price on
screen reads exactly as before, and the tests pass.
